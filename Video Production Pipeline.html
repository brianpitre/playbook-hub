00
Video Director

Bridges the gap between the initial why of the project and the final how. The primary stakeholder who ensures the project meets its goals.

1 Project Initiation & Pre-Production
Creative Briefing
Defining the big picture, target audience, and the desired goal/vision.
Script & Storyboard Approval
Reviewing the script or visual plan to ensure it aligns with the project's objectives.
Talent & Location Scouting
Making the final call on who will be on camera and where the shoot will take place to ensure the visual vibe is correct.
2 Production Support (as needed)
On-Set Direction
While the Production Lead handles the lights and cameras, the Director focuses on the performance—ensuring the subjects are comfortable and delivering the message effectively.
Creative Problem Solving
Acting as the tie-breaker if technical challenges arise.
Accessibility
Remaining available for the Video Editor or Motion Graphics Designer to clarify the intent of a specific scene or graphic style.
3 Post-Production Review & Delivery
Director's Cut Review
Watching the rough cut to provide feedback on pacing, tone, and whether the story is clear.
Quality Control (QC)
Checking for small errors (typos in graphics, audio glitches) that the team might have missed.
Final Sign-off
Giving the green light for the final render and ensuring the video is delivered in the correct formats for its intended platforms.
01
Cinematographer

Responsible for the raw ingredients. If it isn't captured correctly here, it can't be fixed in post—at least not without a headache.

Cinematography & Framing
  • Selecting focal lengths and apertures to match the desired depth of field.
  • Executing camera movement (gimbal, dolly, or handheld) to add energy or focus.
Lighting Design
  • Setting up the primary Key, Fill, and Back lights.
  • Managing Practical lights (on-set lamps/fixtures) and color temperature to ensure skin tones look natural.
Audio Capture
  • Placing lavalier or boom mics for clean dialogue.
  • Monitoring scratch audio on the camera for easier syncing later.
Photography
  • Capturing high-resolution stills for thumbnails, promotional materials, or inserts.
  • Studio lighting and photography for graphics design use.
DIT (Digital Imaging Technician) & Ingest
Ingest
Transferring media from cards to storage.
Organization
Naming folders and files using appropriate naming conventions.
Verification
Using checksums to ensure no data was lost during the transfer.
02
Video Editor

The storyteller who takes the raw assets and finds the soul of the video.

Project Initialization
  • Setting up the project file structure (Footage, Audio, Assets, Renders).
  • Syncing multi-cam clips and external audio tracks.
The Assembly & Rough Cut
Pacing
Selecting the best takes and laying them on the timeline to check the pacing.
B-Roll Integration
Layering visual coverage over talking head or VO segments to illustrate the narrative.
Audio Processing
Cleanup
Cleaning up background noise and applying EQ/Compression so voices sound natural.
Sound Design
Layering ambient sounds and SFX to make the environment feel immersive.
Music Supervision
  • Selecting tracks that match the emotional beat of the video and cutting to the beat where appropriate.
Color Correction
  • Matching different cameras so the look is consistent across the entire edit.
03
Motion Graphics Designer

Adds the professional polish and helps communicate information that isn't captured on camera.

Visual Development
Style Frames
If needed, creating Style Frames (static versions of what the graphics will look like) to get approval before animating.
Brand Consistency
Working with provided brand assets (artwork, logos, fonts, color palettes) to ensure brand consistency.
Titling & Lower Thirds
  • Designing and animating names and titles for subjects appearing on screen.
Graphic Animation
Call-outs & Infographics
Building call-outs, infographics, or other animations to explain complex concepts.
Easing
Applying easing to animations to ensure movement feels natural and fluid rather than robotic.
Compositing
  • Keying green screen footage and layering with backgrounds.
  • Assisting with background cleanup (removing unwanted objects in the background of a shot using tracking).
Final Production
  • Adding SFX for motion graphics.
  • Exporting final versions of videos for review by Video Director and Design Director.
