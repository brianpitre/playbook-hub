About the School

A Global Discipleship Pathway

As The Life Church establishes additional locations across the globe and continues to expand its ministry footprint, the need for a clear and comprehensive discipleship pathway that can develop deeply committed disciples of Jesus and raise up world-class leaders has become increasingly vital.

Life Leadership Bible School is an Online Bible School — a global learning platform that allows all Life Church locations to train and equip their people in Theology, Ministry, and Leadership. This school also formalises the process of ordination, staff training, and team development across the church's network.

About 90% of the content is brand new. It literally adds "Bible School" to "Life Leadership," creating a more fully developed academic program with a higher level of theological and practical ministry training.

Core Outcomes for Students

  • A deeper relationship with Jesus
  • A better grasp of the major doctrinal truths of Scripture
  • A clear view and commitment to God's mission through His Church
  • A solid foundation in biblical leadership principles
  • A greater understanding of holistic self-care
  • A stronger personal leadership culture
  • Practical ministry readiness for those called to full-time ministry
